Closed-Loop Stress Detection Engine

The ESP32 fuses all sensors and only acts when every signal agrees — which avoids false triggers from exercise or a warm room alone.

1 Sense
HR · Temp · Sweat
2 Fuse
All 3 above threshold
3 Act
ESP32 enables atomizer
4 Feedback
Lavender / peppermint, vitals recheck

Auto spray

When HR, temperature and sweat are high at the same time, the ESP32 sets IO23 → EN and the atomizer mists lavender or peppermint oil.

Push-button override

Press the button (IO27) and spraying stops immediately. It stays off until your vitals return to normal, then re-arms.

Closed loop

Sensors keep running during the spray. Once the signals drop below threshold, the spray ends by itself.
